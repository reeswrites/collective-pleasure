# Hedonic technology: the theory this project tests

Date: 2026-11-10. Status: a working theory, not a result. Nothing here is preregistered.

Wording rule: agents feel nothing. "Pleasure" and "suffering" are glosses for a modeled hedonic state (ADR 0010). Every claim about the simulation is about scripted agents with assumed constants, not people.

## 1. The umbrella thesis

**Claim (Claude-added framing, built from the author's notes).** Minds are poor native pursuers of pleasure. Several known gaps sit between what a person does and the pleasure they get over a life.

- **Wanting is not liking.** The chase and the capture run on different mechanisms. The notes say wanting (prediction error) and liking adapt differently, and that overstimulating wanting burns out liking. This is the synthesis's paraphrase of a passage in the note that is a saved AI reply, not the author's own prose (docs/notes/hedonic-technology-synthesis.md, section 1). Do not quote it as the author's words.
- **Memory.** **Author:** "i think the experieicing self is the remembering self and depends onf vividness of memory etc." What a life is worth depends on what is kept and how vividly.
- **Forecasting.** People mispredict what they will like.
- **Discounting.** The near is overweighted.
- **Habit.** Repeated stimuli lose value and people repeat them anyway.

**Hedonic technology** is any structure that closes one of these gaps: a ritual, a norm, a partner, a tool, a rule set. The author's phrase for the relationship case is "relationships as a hedonic technology".

**Care floor.** Each layer of this works once survival is secured elsewhere. The author's unbundling note says to build care first, because losing care is the real danger. So the floor sits under the technology and is not bought with pleasure. How a hedonist grounds the floor is open (section 4).

**Why the project cares.** Rule sets are hedonic technology for a household. If minds were perfect pursuers, rules would only need to prevent harm. Because they are imperfect, a rule set can also produce pleasure, and that is the author's condition for seed v2.2: "yes as long as it can demonstrate pleasure in relationships hedonic technology".

## 2. Three tests of good hedonic technology

The first test is **Author** (the notes). The second is the author's "derived from vs extracted from" test, restated in the conversation. The third came out of the reassurance-seeking part of the conversation. Putting the three side by side as a test set is **Claude-added**. They become evaluator criteria in ADR 0018.

1. **Liking, not only wanting.** Does the technology raise the pleasure that is felt, or only the pull toward more? A metrics list on the author's engineering page says only closure rate and next-day verdict measure liking. [Correction 2026-11-11: that list and claim are from likely AI-written text on the author's Notion page, which the author does not hold. Closure rate and next-day verdict are Claude's measures of liking.] A fast system can drift into the trap with no metric noticing.
2. **Persists because worth it, not because hard to leave.** Why does it persist? If the outputs are worth the upkeep, the technology works. If leaving costs too much, it is extraction. The author's relationship note says both people should be better off and free to leave.
3. **Lasting security, not access you must keep checking.** Does it settle something, or does it need constant reassurance to feel safe? The author raised reassurance seeking (googling, AI, Reddit, advice) as a case. **Claude (paraphrase):** the loop never closes, because relief keeps it running and the fear is never tested.

## 3. Relationships as hedonic technology

Everything in this section is the author's thesis extended in the conversation. Where a step is Claude's, it is marked.

### The firm with three product lines (Claude-added framing)

**Claude** proposed that a relationship is a firm with three product lines. The author then corrected the structure: burden sharing and interpretation or regulation work on different levels. The author said regulation and interpretation are more about maintaining the relationship, and "not just done for its own" sake.

- **Shared pleasure.** Rewards similarity: a third thing both enjoy, loops, and the amplification of an activity by doing it together. **Author**, 2026-11-10: "if you like eating with others then it makes sense that it could amplify or channel pleasure". This is the source of the horizon study's amplification amendment (docs/results/horizon-prereg.md).
- **Burden sharing and specialization.** Rewards difference. **Author:** "burden sharing in order to free up time energy space etc to be able to pursue pleausre solely or with the other perosn". Burden sharing makes slack, and slack is spent on solo pleasure (replaceable) or joint pleasure (only this person). The author also noted that a burden can be a cost or, for some people, suffering, so sharing can give slack or relief. **Claude:** pooling across tasks, time and uncertainty is one mechanism.
- **Regulation and interpretation.** The loops that let two people read and steady each other. It is upkeep and it also does pleasure work: being understood is itself a pleasure, and readability raises the gain of pleasure loops (**Claude**).

### Relational load, sender and receiver

**Author:** the labor is "hermeneutic labor cognitive labor emotional labor", and the author preferred "load" to "labor" in an earlier note. **Claude-added** synthesis: three layers, each with a sender job and a receiver job.

| Layer | Sender | Receiver |
|---|---|---|
| Interpretive | make yourself readable | read the other |
| Affective | notice and regulate your own activation | tolerate what you read |
| Logistical | hold your own planning, offer defaults | track needs over time |

**Conservation (Claude-added, from the author's notes).** Load is conserved. What one partner skips lands on the other as decoding, vigilance or appeasement. The author's earlier line is the same claim: neurotype explains why something costs more and does not settle who should pay for it. Volume is how much load exists. Asymmetry is who carries it.

### Attunement and appeasement

**Author:** the two are "all about mood management in a relationship". **Claude** pushed back: they share an input (reading the other's state) and differ in the controlled variable. Appeasement controls the other person's mood. Attunement takes the mood as information and controls something higher, such as connection or honesty.

**Claude-added test:** an attuned person can say no or raise a hard thing and tolerate the displeasure. An appeaser structurally cannot. **Author** supplied the receiver-side piece, noticing your own activation and tolerating a family member's negative emotion, and Claude noted that the notes were thin on it.

**The link to this project.** Appeasement trades long-run pleasure for less immediate pain. That is the same greedy pattern the horizon study measured: a policy that looks only at the reward it can see loses to a planner whenever actions leave a trace in state (docs/results/horizon.md, sections 1 and 3). Appeasement is the relationship version. The trace is the suppressed signal, which Claude likened to a leak in a stable loop.

### Support from the literature: Sinhababu (added 2026-11-11, at the author's request)

Claude offered two points from Neil Sinhababu, and the author said "yeah add both". Whether the author first asked about Sinhababu is not logged. The summaries below come from secondary sources checked by web search on 2026-11-11. They are Claude's summaries. Read the primary texts before citing.

- **The epistemic argument for hedonism** (Sinhababu 2015; a later version in a 2024 volume; a guest essay on utilitarianism.net). Moral disagreement undermines most moral beliefs, because the processes that form them are unreliable. Phenomenal introspection is the exception. It is reliable for nonmoral facts about experience, and it reveals that pleasure is good and displeasure is bad. So the goodness of pleasure survives the skepticism. This supports the author's position that pleasure is the good. It also bears on the "thermometer, not temperature" reading. That image is from a saved AI reply under the author's note title "pleasure as the detector of value" (docs/notes/hedonic-technology-synthesis.md, Q13). It is not the author's words, and the synthesis lists it as an open tension for the author, not a worry the author holds. Pleasure's goodness is given in the experience itself, and what misfires is desire and consequence. A known objection: if pleasure is defined as "felt goodness", the claim may be analytic.
- **Amplification by vividness** (Sinhababu, *Humean Nature*, OUP 2017). Desire drives action, and its effects grow with the vividness of sensory or imaginative representations of what is desired. This explains procrastination: a vivid temptation beats a judged-more-important but faint goal.

**How the model uses this (Claude-added framing).** Vividness does two jobs that can come apart:
- At decision time, vividness amplifies wanting (Sinhababu).
- Afterward, memory vividness weights liking in the remembering self (the author's view, ADR 0017).

The middle reading of the detector worry is: liking is the good, and wanting is the detector that misfires. This is Claude's synthesis, offered to the author, not yet confirmed. Candidate hypothesis H34 tests vivid cues that inflate wanting without liking.

## 4. The author's one-self view and lifetime pleasure

**Author:** "lifetime pleasure seems obvious to me", and "i think the experieicing self is the remembering self". ADR 0017 records the measure: the sum over moments of present pleasure, vividness-weighted memory re-experienced now, and anticipation. Greedy scoring is the special case that is exact only when nothing carries over.

The author also said "idk if we can prove that somehow". The horizon study proves a narrower model result: greedy is optimal for every reward and every discount exactly when actions leave no trace the reward sees. That supports the author's hunch in the model; it does not prove it for people. Discounting does not remove the need to plan. In the model, planning starts to matter at a discount of 0.378.

This is the formal home for the umbrella thesis. Memory, anticipation, adaptation and bonds are the carried-over state that makes a greedy pursuer worse than a planner. A hedonic technology works by moving a pursuer's choices toward the planner's.

Still open and the author's to decide: how the care floor is grounded (security for both ahead of time, or a commitment outside the pleasure sum), how strongly memory counts against the present, and whether a short intense life can beat a long mild one.

## 5. Where the simulation stands

| Claim | Modeled | Not modeled | Evidence and limits |
|---|---|---|---|

| Greedy loses to a planner | Proved, and measured at 0 to 25 percent by discount | Real agents' bias; household personas are scripted | docs/results/horizon.md |

| Unbundled networks, thick core | Lever L-C | Care-first ordering, regulation across links | ADR 0016 |
| Shared pleasure, amplification | Horizon worked case, amended | Amplification in the household world | docs/results/horizon-prereg.md |
| Free to leave, negotiated terms, floor | Negotiation, exit floor, outside options | Terms inside the pair barely move with outside options | docs/results/negotiation.md, negotiation-v2.1.md; fidelity P1a |
| Rules produce pleasure, not only protect | Pleasure demonstration | The effect was small and the relational share came from persona weights | docs/results/pleasure-demo.md |
| Burden sharing, specialization | Shared pot only | Tasks, comparative advantage, relief versus slack | none |
| Relational load, sender and receiver | Nothing | The whole layer | none |
| Attunement versus appeasement | Not as a policy; pressing personas and exit exist | Regulation policy, mood coupling | H01, H24 |
| Reassurance seeking, checking cost | Nothing | A cost of access you must keep checking | H28 |

**What the fidelity battery says about the limits.** Household results must not lean on behavioral findings (stickiness of defaults, fine crowd-out, adaptation with levers off). The base model is a rational-agent model, so it fails those tests by construction (docs/results/fidelity.md). Claims about imperfect pursuers need agents that are imperfect on purpose.
