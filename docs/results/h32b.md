# H32b results: the voice ladder as specified does not help; withdrawal alone does, and renegotiation hurts

Wording rule: agents feel nothing. "Pleasure" is a gloss for a modeled hedonic state (ADR 0010). Every number is ASSUMED. Findings are about one small model, not people.

Status: confirmatory run of docs/results/h32b-prereg.md (commit 551d099, pushed before any solve of the five-activity menu with the ladder; no amendment, no threshold moved). Exact dynamic programming, no seeds, no model calls, $0. Code: `src/mar/convergence.py` (`ConvParams.ladder`, off by default), tests `tests/test_convergence.py` (11 pass, including a regression test against the committed H32 numbers), script `scripts/h32b_run.py`. Run: 1,502 solves, 7 minutes on 2 workers. Report: `experiments/kept/h32b-2026-11-11/report.html`; numbers in `results.json` beside it. Only tests/test_convergence.py was run before commits (coordinator instruction; the full suite is locked).

**Verdict: the full ladder does not make the egoist converge anywhere the cliff did not.** It made it converge in 0 of 30 cells, against the cliff's 1. 6 of 8 predictions held. The rung decomposition holds the surprise: **withdrawal alone** beats the cliff and converges in 2 cells, while **renegotiation** undoes most of what withdrawal does. Check-in only adds delay, and delay helps the egoist.

## Which rung does the work (exit+amp arm, giving off)

| alpha 1, o_B 0.6 | 0.19 / 0.086 (partner leaves) | 0.25 / 0.113 | 0.50 / 0.226 | **0.02 / 0.009 (pair kept)** | 0.50 / 0.226 | 0.25 / 0.113 | 0.50 / 0.226 | 0.50 / 0.226 |

Mean rung Shapley on D over 30 cells (positive = closes the gap relative to the cliff): check-in -0.006, renegotiate -0.016, withdraw +0.002. In the cell alpha 1, o_B 0.6: check-in -0.058, renegotiate -0.308, withdraw +0.058.

What happens, in words:
- **Withdrawal** is a punishment the egoist feels. When the partner withdraws, shared weeks lose half their amplification, and amplification is why this egoist values the pair. So the egoist keeps the partner off that rung by giving it common-ground weeks. With withdrawal as the only rung, the egoist converges in both cells, and the pair stays together, which the cliff could not do at o_B 0.6 (there the partner left).
- **Renegotiation** as built is a demand the egoist can meet cheaply. The demand applies only on the renegotiation rung, and the egoist's next planned week (common ground B) already meets it. The partner then calms down one rung, and the egoist goes back to its own passion. Renegotiation turns the partner's distress into a predictable, satisfiable rule, and the egoist plays it exactly. That is what the prereg feared about the full ladder, and it is the renegotiation rung that does it.
- **Check-in** carries no information here (the actor already sees the partner's estimate). It only adds a week before anything bites, and that week is slack for the egoist.

## Robustness (same checks as H32, on the 15 cells with alpha <= 1)

Voice-driven cells, full ladder: by partner sight w = 1, 0.75, 0.5, 0.25, 0 at noise 0: 0, 1, 0, 0, 0. At w = 1 with noise 0.05, 0.1, 0.2: 0, 0, 0. The H32 cliff on the same cells: 1, 0, 1, 1, 0. Neither mechanism gives a robust result. The withdraw-only subset was not part of the robustness sweep (the prereg ran it on the full ladder only), so its 2 cells are untested for noise and blindness.

## Plain-English reading

The author's answer, "not leaving right away but ultimately what the person does is the strongest signal", fits the result in an unexpected way. In this model, what moves a self-interested partner is a consequence it feels: withdrawal takes away the shared pleasure it was in the pair for. Talking (check-in) changes nothing when the other already knows how you feel. A renegotiated demand that is met week by week can be gamed: the egoist does exactly what the rule asks when the rule is active and nothing more. And "not leaving right away" hands the egoist time. So for the founding question (can a mechanism make a self-interested agent attend to another's welfare?), the useful mechanism here is graded withdrawal of the thing the agent values, not voice and not a cliff exit.

This is one cell pair in one menu. The withdraw-only finding was not a preregistered headline (it came out of the preregistered rung decomposition) and needs its own prereg and robustness check before it is cited.

## ASSUMED and limits

All of H32's, plus: rung thresholds at o_B + 0.3, 0.2, 0.1; rung level moves one step a week both ways; renegotiation demand is "a shared activity the partner likes at least as much as the actor"; withdrawal removes half the amplification bonus. A ladder that calms down more slowly than it escalates, or a demand over a window of weeks rather than one week, could change the renegotiation result; neither was swept. Full information means the check-in has no job; a partially observed model would give it one.
