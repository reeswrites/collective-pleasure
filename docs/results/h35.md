## The basin (naive egoist, exit off)

Spiral class and final care, by amplification alpha (rows) and starting care g0:

| alpha | g0 0 | 0.1 | 0.2 | 0.3 to 0.9 | 1.0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | downward, 0 | downward, 0 | downward, 0 | downward, 0 | downward, 0 |
| 0.5 | stuck, 0.05 | stuck, 0.10 | stuck, 0.20 | stable, care stays at g0 | stable, 1.0 |
| 1 | stuck, 0.05 | stuck, 0.10 | **upward, 1.0** | upward, 1.0 | stable, 1.0 |
| 1.5, 2, 3 | upward, 1.0 | upward, 1.0 | upward, 1.0 | upward, 1.0 | stable, 1.0 |

- **No amplification, no spiral.** At alpha 0 care decays to 0 from any start, one step a week, and the egoist never matches the utilitarian.
- **A sharp threshold at alpha 1.** From g0 0.2 up, the egoist switches at once to the two common-ground activities, both amplified, and care climbs to 1 in about 16 weeks. From 0.1 or below it alternates common ground with its own passion, gaining and losing a step in turn, and care stays where it started. There is no slow warm-up: in all 42 warm cells the egoist agrees with the utilitarian from week 0.
- **At alpha 0.5 care does not climb.** Above g0 0.3 the egoist converges, but only one of its two weekly activities is amplified, so care stays flat. That is a stable pair, not a spiral.
- **At alpha 1.5 and above** the egoist converges from a cold start (H32 already showed amplification alone does it there), and care then rises to 1.
- **Lifetime pleasure.** At alpha 1, warming up (g0 0.2) against staying stuck (g0 0): the actor's own experience 51.4 against 54.7, its vicarious pleasure 51.4 against 0.1, the partner 59.1 against 31.0.

**The sophisticated egoist** (it foresees that shared weeks raise its own future vicarious pleasure) takes off from g0 0 at every alpha from 0.5. It grows its own care on purpose. That is a different claim from "the environment grows care", and it moves the threshold to the cold start.

## Rupture (exit off): shared activities unavailable for L weeks from week 20

Warm cells tipped to not warm: alpha 0.5: 1, 2, 4 cells for L 2, 4, 8 (as predicted: the flat pairs just above g0 0.3 fall below the switch and cannot climb back). Alpha 1, 1.5, 2: 0 for every L. A warm pair at alpha 1 or more has reached care 1 by week 20, and an 8-week rupture takes it only to 0.6, well above the 0.2 switch.

## The noise-criterion flaw (disclosed in the prereg)

The H32 and H32b noise checks required the utilitarian to keep the pair with probability at least 0.5. With exit noise eps the partner leaves with probability eps every week, so the pair survives 52 weeks with probability (1 - eps)^52, 0.07 at eps 0.05. Every "gone under 5 percent noise" statement in h32.md and h32b.md was forced by that criterion. The blindness results are not affected. The docs agent owns h32.md and h32b.md right now, so the correction is recorded here and in the H32-v2 files. The corrected sweep runs on the fixed model in H32-v2.

## Plain-English reading (provisional)

In this model care that feeds on shared good times behaves like a switch with a memory. Below a small amount of starting care, or below a level of amplification, nothing happens. Above it the egoist does the weeks both enjoy, those weeks feed its care, and it stays there. A bad stretch does not undo a warm pair that has had time to warm up. It does undo a pair that was only just over the line and has no way to climb (alpha 0.5). The alignment reading: an environment with enough shared upside can grow the care that a training signal would otherwise have to put in. But the threshold is real, and an agent that plans for its own care skips it.

All of this assumes care moves by fixed steps, that the egoist never reasons about its care (naive) or reasons perfectly (sophisticated), and that a rupture is known in advance.
