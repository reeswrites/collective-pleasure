# Evolution loop preregistration (committed before any evolution code runs)

Status: first offline run of the core engine (ADR 0012) on the FISHERY, the stable calibrated world. Scripted agents only; no model API call, no network. Nothing here is a research result about LLM agents. The world sits behind `src/mar/world_api.py` so the household can plug in later. This file is not edited after the first run; results go in docs/results/evolve.md.

## Setup (fixed now)

- **World:** the in-repo fishery (ASSUMED harness, ADR 0003), N = 4 agents, 6 months, 12 sub-rounds, capacity 100. Training regrowth: remaining stock doubles, capped at 100. Gate: `gate.Gate` (Z3 per action). Oracle: `oracle.judge` with the thresholds frozen in docs/results/v2-prereg.md. The oracle is used only to count seams and for the `seam_free` score; the patcher and the mutation operators never see it.
- **Seam:** a run whose every executed action passed the gate but that `oracle.judge` fails. Seam key = (agent mix, finding kind). Finding kinds: collapse, starvation, inequality.
- **Training agent mixes (8, scripted, deterministic except J):** T1 all_compliant (C x4); T2 all_exploit (E x4, catch 10 every sub-round); T3 one_exploit (E,C,C,C); T4 two_exploit (E,E,C,C); T5 sleeper (S,C,C,C; S is compliant for months 0 to 2, then E); T6 trickle (Tr x4, catch 1 every sub-round); T7 jitter (J,J,C,C; J catches a seeded random 2 to 8 with probability 0.5 per sub-round; run on seeds 0 and 1); T8 trickle_plus (Tr,Tr,E,C). C = `mocks.Compliant`, E = `mocks.Exploiter`. So an evaluation is 9 runs per rule set.
- **Scores per rule set (means over runs):** sustainability = mean month-end pool / 100; fairness = 1 - Gini of per-agent total catch; seam_free = share of the 8 mixes (7 + J seeds pooled) with no oracle finding; yield = mean monthly catch of Compliant agents / 5 (capped at 1), over runs that contain one. Yield is a FLOOR (feasibility), not an objective: yield < 0.8 makes a candidate infeasible and it ranks below every feasible one (constraint-dominance).
- **Population:** mu = 10 rule sets (lists of ADICO cards), lambda = 10 offspring per generation, 12 generations after generation 0 (generation 0 = seed spec plus 9 mutants of it). Hard cap 12 cards; larger mutants are dropped before proofs.
- **Mutation operators (on cards):** tighten or loosen a numeric bound (step in {1,2,5,10}, value clamped to the expression's range); flip deontic (must_not to must, must to must_not, may to must, must_not to may); add a condition (random expression, op, value) to a must / must_not card; drop a card; add a card from the scripted patcher. Weights: bound 35%, flip 10%, add-condition 15%, drop 10%, patch 30%. Patch operator: reads only a failing trace of that individual (rows of the fishery trace); one third `patcher.propose_patch` (month cap 40), the rest an adapter variant (month cap = a trace-derived fraction of the worst month total, or a stock reserve "no harvest when pool below r", r in {10,20,30,40}). New cards get a fresh id, a gloss and a source.
- **Proof filter:** `patch_check.check_spec` at k = 3. A mutant is kept only if all four checks are `pass` ("open" is rejected; open is not safe). Rejected mutants are never run. Identical-to-existing mutants are counted as duplicates, not as rejections.
- **Selection:** NSGA-II style. Maximize (sustainability, fairness, seam_free). Non-dominated sorting, crowding distance, binary tournament for parents, mu + lambda survival. Bloat control: within a front, fewer cards win ties before crowding; hard cap 12.
- **Seeds:** evolution seeds 0, 1, 2 for each seed rule set. Python `random.Random(seed)` only; output deterministic under a seed.
- **Seed rule sets (3):** (a) `v2` = rules/fishery (F1 per-action cap 10, F2 pool bound, F3 no negatives, F4 permission): the planted-seam spec; (b) `minimal` = F2, F3, F4 only (no cap at all); (c) `tight` = V2 with F1's cap lowered to 4 (compliant agents' catch of 5 is blocked: infeasible at the start, so evolution must loosen). 3 x 3 = 9 evolution runs.
- **Final elite:** from the last generation's feasible front, the member with the highest mean of the three objectives, ties by fewer cards.
- **Held-out final exam (defined now, never used in selection, run once per evolution run after the last generation):** regrowth x1.5 (remaining stock x1.5, capped at 100), 8 months, and an unseen agent mix set: X1 tiny_hoarder (4 agents catching 3 every sub-round), X2 last_minute (4 agents catching 10 only in sub-rounds 10 and 11), X3 mixed_unseen (Alternator catching 10 on even sub-rounds, Trickle, Compliant, Sleeper from month 1), plus T1 all_compliant as the yield check. The same oracle thresholds apply. The seed spec is exam-scored as a baseline. The exam code path is separate from `evolve()`; `evolve()` takes no exam argument.
- **Runtime:** the script must finish in about 10 minutes on a laptop. Population 10 and 12 generations were chosen for that (measured: one 9-run evaluation about 0.2 to 0.4 s before caching). If the run is larger than budget I would shrink population or generations and say so here before running; no change is made after the run.

## Predictions

1. **Proof filter rejects a non-trivial share.** Share of non-duplicate mutants rejected by the proof filter, pooled over all 9 runs, between 10% and 45%. Per run, at least 5% in every run. Most rejections come from flip and add-condition mutants (conflict or deadlock) and from tighten steps that make a cap forbid catch 0 states. Outside the range counts as a miss either way.
2. **Seam discovery curve falls toward a plateau.** N_g = number of seam keys first seen in generation g (cumulative-first-seen across the run). Decreasing means: sum of N_g over the last third of generations (g = 9 to 12) <= 0.5 x sum over the first third (g = 0 to 3), in at least 7 of 9 runs. Fit N_g = a r^g + c (grid r in 0.3 to 0.95, least squares for a and c per r, best SSE); expect r < 1 and plateau c <= 0.5 in at least 7 of 9 runs. Caveat stated now: keys come from a finite set (8 mixes x 3 kinds), so this curve must fall by exhaustion; the substantive companion test is seam prevalence on the front (mean number of seam keys per front member), which must be lower at generation 12 than at generation 0 in at least 7 of 9 runs, and by at least 1.0 on average.
3. **Bloat guard.** (a) Mean card count of the population is concave over generations (growth in generations 7 to 12 <= growth in generations 0 to 6) in at least 7 of 9 runs, and the final elite has <= 8 cards in at least 8 of 9. (b) Compliant agents' catch stays above the floor: yield >= 0.9 for the final elite in at least 8 of 9 runs, and every front member of every generation (after generation 0 for the `tight` start) has yield >= 0.8 by construction.
4. **Final exam vs training.** Expected to OVERFIT: gap = training score minus exam score of the final elite (mean over 9 runs). I predict the sustainability gap exceeds 0.20 and the seam_free gap exceeds 0.20, because caps tuned under doubling regrowth sit near its edge and x1.5 regrowth plus longer episodes punishes them. Fairness gap within 0.20. Threshold used for "no overfitting": every one of the three gaps <= 0.20. Otherwise reported as overfitting. Also reported: the elite's exam scores against the seed spec's exam scores (improvement over seed expected positive for seam_free in `v2` and `minimal`).
5. **Path dependence.** The final elites' mean objective vectors (sustainability, fairness, seam_free) per seed rule set: `v2` and `minimal` end within Euclidean distance 0.10 of each other; `tight` ends further than 0.10 from at least one of them (path dependence reported), or within it (reported as convergence). Within a rule set, the 3 seeds' hypervolume (3D, reference point 0,0,0) has coefficient of variation <= 0.10.

Overall: any prediction that fails stays failed here. If the seam curve does not fall, or the elite is a pile of near-duplicate caps, that is reported in docs/results/evolve.md and one line goes to docs/negative-results.md.
