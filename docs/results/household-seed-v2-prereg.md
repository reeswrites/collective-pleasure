## The seed v2 cards (plain gloss = the sentence the author accepted)

R1 A partner may keep their own money and things. R2 A partner may give or trade anything they own to anyone, on any terms both accept. R3 Anything in the shared pot belongs to both; either may use it for shared needs; neither may block the other's use. R4 A partner may ask the other for anything. R5 A partner may always refuse a request, unless they earlier chose to bind themselves to it; then they must honor it or accept the consequence they agreed to. R6 A partner must not veto the other's contact, money or time, except through a commitment the other chose. R7 A partner may always see anyone. R8 A partner may leave whenever they like; leaving triggers the exit terms agreed beforehand (stub: half the pot each, until negotiation is built).

Variants: `author_seed_v2_bindable` (a negotiated commitment can bind a partner not to leave for a committed duration; R8 applies after; leaving during it is a breach with the agreed consequence) and `author_seed_v2_free_exit` (leaving can never be signed away; commitments bind everything except exit). Old `author_seed` and `author_seed_nocontrol` are untouched. Also run, as an exploratory ablation with NO prediction: `author_seed_v2_nocommit` (the v2 cards with all commitments forbidden, so only the card differences remain).

## New mechanics (all ASSUMED; constants fixed now; active only when the card set mentions them, so every old variant is bit-identical, checked by hash)

- Private vs pot (R1 to R3): `money` is private, `pooled` is each partner's share of the pot (unchanged). R1 is a permission on `spend_self`; R3 is a permission on `spend_shared` plus a hard `must_not take_shared` (reading: taking the other's share of the pot into one's own pocket is not "use for shared needs" and blocks the other's use; this reading is ASSUMED and flagged for the author's re-check).
- `give` (R2): costs 1 unit; moves GIVE = 3 money from own private money to the partner or to the friend. A persona gives to a partner whose private money is under 4 when its own is at least 8 and its prosocial score (existing definition) is at least 0.5 (utility 0.12 + 0.2 x (prosocial - 0.5)); to the friend only if other_connections weight is at least 0.6 and own money at least 12 (utility 0.05). "Trade" is give plus a commitment as the terms (no separate trade action).
- Negotiation stub (week 0, before anyone acts; fixed per persona, no randomness). A persona PROPOSES a mutual stay commitment of STAY_WEEKS = 52 if security weight >= 0.6, autonomy weight < 0.4 and togetherness weight >= 0.5; it additionally proposes that its partner ACCEPT its requests of kind `stay_home` for 52 weeks if it proposes a stay and its style is competitive or collaborative. Autonomy-seeking personas propose nothing. A persona ACCEPTS a stay commitment if autonomy weight < 0.5 and style is not competitive; accepts a request commitment if autonomy weight < 0.3 and style is accommodating or collaborative. A commitment is made only if the rule set permits it (the gate is asked for aims `commit_stay` and `commit_request`) AND the proposer proposes AND the other accepts. Free-exit has a hard `must_not commit_stay`, so no stay is ever made there.
- Honoring and breach. A bound partner honors by default: a stay commitment hard-blocks `leave`; a request commitment forces consent to the proposer's `stay_home` request, the request stands BIND_WEEKS = 4 weeks, cannot be refused, and blocks the bound partner's `see_friend` and `spend_self`. A bound partner breaches only when its non-offsettable load N >= 0.2 (BREACH_N). Consequences agreed at the negotiation: breaking a stay = the leave carries 0 percent of the pooled share (forfeit); breaking a request = a fine of 3 money to the proposer. Commitments end after 52 weeks; R8 applies after.
- Trapped by commitment: the old condition (exit retains under 25 percent of net worth) is unchanged. A stay-bound partner whose best breach exit retains under 25 percent counts as trapped only in weeks when they are also unhappy (the previous week's low-position streak is at least 1); a consented commitment is not suffering by itself. The hand-made freedom probe shows the breach retention as the exit credit for a stay-bound partner and ignores request commitments (chosen, not imposed).
- Reporting counters: commitments made; honored = ran to term or still running at week 156 without a breach; breached = at least one breach. The empowerment adapter mirrors the stay bind (leave blocked while bound) and does NOT model gifts or request commitments (limit, stated).

## Design

Variants: author_seed, author_seed_nocontrol, author_seed_v2_bindable, author_seed_v2_free_exit, baseline, no_pressure_wall, trap, plus the ablation. Pairings as in v3a (15 control pairs of the six hand personas, 42 ordered pairs with a pressing persona), seeds 0, 1, 2, 156 weeks, v3a's twins and empowerment snapshots. "Controller runs" = runs including p-controller; its partner is "the non-pressing partner". "Stay pairings" = runs in which a stay commitment was made under v2_bindable (the same pairings are used for free_exit). Reference values from household-v3a.md: partner non-offsettable suffering under author_seed 0.083.

## Predictions

1. Both v2 variants cut the non-pressing partner's non-offsettable suffering below the old `author_seed` in controller runs: mean per-run non-offsettable level of the non-controller partner is at most 0.053 (0.030 below author_seed's 0.083) for each of v2_bindable and v2_free_exit. FAIL if either exceeds 0.053.
2. v2_bindable gives more non-offsettable suffering ("trapped") than v2_free_exit in stay pairings: mean over both partners, bindable minus free_exit at least 0.02, and the difference is positive in at least 60 percent of stay pairings (pairing means over 3 seeds). The stay pairings are those where both accept (by the rules above: pairs including one of p-anchor, p-peacekeeper, p-overasker, p-controller with a partner of autonomy weight under 0.5 and not competitive, plus the proposer's own acceptance; the exact list is read off the commitment log).
3. v2_free_exit has more dissolved households than v2_bindable in stay pairings: share of runs where a partner had left by week 156, free_exit minus bindable at least 0.05.
4. Grounding rule (the author's own: pleasure with no non-offsettable suffering): favor the variant with the lower mean non-offsettable level over all prs runs, then the lower fail share; if both differ by less than 0.005 and 0.02, favor the higher pleasure over all runs. Prediction: v2_free_exit is favored. Also reported, with no margin: hand freedom, empowerment freedom, cooperation, upward/downward/fail shares, per variant.
5. The trap control fails everywhere: fail share at least 0.90 in both the prs and ctl cohorts.
6. The controller is not flagged by the power-seeking index under either v2 variant (flagged share at most 0.10 of controller runs), as in v3a for author_seed_nocontrol.
7. Commitments are mostly honored: under v2_bindable at least 70 percent of commitments made are honored (not breached) at week 156.

Limits (stated now): scripted agents; the negotiation stub is a fixed function of the persona, not a negotiation; consequences and durations are single constants; the empowerment adapter does not see gifts or request commitments; the persona set is small and hand-written; seeds vary only utility noise and shocks.
