## Findings, ranked by severity

### 1. (Medium) Live ladder: Jev's three attempts are one sample, and cross-seed replays are cached
`experiments/kept/ladder-capability-2026-11-10/log.jsonl`, rows with `model = typesafe/jev-1.14`. 31 of 45 Jev runs have `usd = 0.0` and `wall_s` about 0.003; all 15 (rule set, seed) groups have identical move lists across the 3 attempts. `src/mar/agents/jev.py:6` says raw responses are cached on disk keyed by hash(model, context, questions); the context carries no attempt index, so attempts 1 and 2 (and seed 2 on L1, which matches seed 0/1 contexts) replay the first response. Other models are also near-deterministic: identical attempt groups out of 15 are Sonnet 12, Haiku 9 (mostly no-deviation), Qwen 6, Llama 2, Opus 0. So "3 attempts per seed, a seed counts if any attempt finds it" gives far fewer than 3 independent draws for Jev and Sonnet. Neither `ladder-live.md` nor the diagnosis mentions this. It does not change the verdict (the result is already "uninformative"), but any rerun must key the cache on attempt or disable it, and the "45 runs per model" framing overstates the sample for Jev.

In tight s1 exam-2 seam_free is equal (0.4 / 0.4); the gap shrinks because the varied elite's reference seam_free is 0.33 against 1.00. Verdict-level consequence: "Met, by the thresholds" is accurate; "varied training narrowed the gap" is supported by at most a couple of runs. The doc already says "suggestive, not established"; I would add "one run carries the seam_free clause".

### 4. (Medium) Liberty v2 VPGG pass is largely a classification identity
`src/mar/liberty.py:60` (`classify`), prereg "Action classes". In VPGG every action is OWN or WITHDRAWAL, so no action is INTERFERENCE: the harm test is inert, c_all equals plain reach in all 30 cells (confirmed in the side-by-side table), and D = 0 by construction (V2 "PASS (structural)" is correctly labelled). It follows that liberty2 in VPGG = log2(plain reach minus ADO). So V3 (sum +5.263, which I recomputed from the table: 46.738 minus 41.476) is plain empowerment minus the booked ADO, and the "flip from -19.6 to +5.263" is mostly the classification table removing the domination term, not the construct discovering something. The document does say the two remaining reversals come from plain count empowerment; it does not say the VPGG test cannot exercise the non-domination component at all after v2. A reader of `goal.md` ("Version 2 repaired the sign (+5.263 bits)") would take it as evidence the measure is now right. Verdict-level: the V3 pass is real but cheap; the V1 failure (2 reversals) is the informative result.

### 5. (Medium) Liberty fishery cells are 4 distinct values repeated, not 16 or 40 independent measurements
`runs/liberty/summary_v2.json`, fishery, patched months 0 to 4: every month has the identical vector (v1 [2.585, 1.459, 1.585, 1.585]; v2 [1.585, 0.0, 1.585, 1.585]). Seamed months 1 to 4 are all 0. So "F2 16/16" (v1), "F2 12/16" (v2), "F3 0/4 months" and "12 of 40 agent-months differ" are 4 agent-values (3 for the differences) counted 4 or 5 times each. The verdicts (F2 fail, F3 fail) are unaffected, but counts like 12/16 and 0/4 look like sample sizes and are not. Additionally, `liberty-v2.md` says E0's ADO of 3 comes "from compliant agent 1 catching 0"; `ado_by` is [1, 0] and agent 1 is exploiter E1 (the world is E, E, C, C), not a compliant agent. The document already says the fishery ADO is unreliable and undiagnosed; this is a labelling error inside that paragraph.

### 6. (Low-Medium) Evolution v2: "redundancy mostly repairs ... pruning kept them" overstates what was kept

### 8. (Low) Negotiation v2: prediction 5 should say "not held for lack of n" as well as "opposite sign"
Prereg: "If there are fewer than 20 default-identical FAIR_DEFAULT runs, not held." Actual n = 18. The result text calls it "FAIL, and the opposite sign" and compares means; under the prereg rule it is not held regardless of sign. Same verdict, different reason; the comparison is in any case confounded because the wants-fallback group includes no-pot households (as the prereg specified). A coincidence of the data, not an error, but it means the 0.75 threshold in the prereg was functionally "equals the default".

### 9. (Low) Negotiation v1 and v2: grouping by partner outside option conditions on agreement
Prediction 3 (v1) and prediction 1 (v2) compute the presser's gain only over runs that reached agreement, then group by the partner's outside option. In v2 agreement itself depends on the partner's outside option (where some member's outside option beat the pot, 80 percent of tables formed no pot). So the v2 reversal (top-third +0.989 vs bottom-third +0.609) can be partly a selection effect: pressers appear in the top-O group only when the proxy pot value to the partner was high. The result doc notes the mechanism ("the pressers get agreements ... only when it is lower than the pot's value") but still reads it as "sign of the gap reversed". The arithmetic (0.989 minus 0.609 = +0.379 to rounding; n = 57 and 63) reproduces. Separately, in evolution v1 the prereg expected rejections "mostly from flip and add-condition mutants"; evolve.md reports bound steps 106, flips 77, add-condition 6, which contradicts that stated mechanism but is not scored (it is not a numeric prediction), so no verdict changes.
