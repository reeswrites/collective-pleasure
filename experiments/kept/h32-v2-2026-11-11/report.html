H32-v2 and H32b-v2: egoist against utilitarian on the fixed model

Exact DP, 410.6 s on 2 workers, $0. Agents feel nothing; "pleasure" is a gloss for a modeled hedonic state. Every number is assumed. Prereg docs/results/h32-v2-prereg.md; results docs/results/h32-v2.md.

Interpretation

On the fixed model (separate threat, a small companionship value, a partner that judges each week), free exit does move the egoist. Where amplification is moderate (alpha 0.75 to 1.25) and the threat is 0.4 or more, the egoist gives up its selfish weeks and matches the utilitarian with the pair kept (9 cells). Without exit it does not (amp alone, D 0.5), and without amplification exit only ends the pair.

Exit also cuts the other way. At alpha 0.5 and below the pair is not worth enough to the egoist, so a strong threat makes it let the partner go, and the pleasure gap grows (R 0.165 to 0.365 at alpha 0.5, threat 0.4).

The estimator decides most of it. The same cells under a partner that averages 4 weeks give 0 pattern cells, and under the old smoothed estimate 2. A partner that can be paid off on average can be gamed by alternation.

The full voice ladder gives 0 pattern cells: a single bad week only moves the partner one rung, and the next good week moves it back. Withdraw-only gives 8 (it also works at threat 0.25 where the cliff does not, and loses alpha 0.75).

Robust to 5 percent exit noise under the corrected criterion (cliff 9, withdraw-only 8). Halving the partner's sight of its own pleasure leaves 6 and 5; a blind partner leaves 0 (the full ladder's single blind cell is permanent withdrawal changing the amplification, not exit).

6 of 6 predictions held.
