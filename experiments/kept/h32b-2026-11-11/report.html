H32b: does a graded voice ladder make the egoist converge where cliff exit did not?

Exact dynamic programming, 1502 solves, 414.2 s on 2 workers, $0. Agents feel nothing; "pleasure" is a gloss for a modeled hedonic state. Every number is assumed. Prereg docs/results/h32b-prereg.md; results docs/results/h32b.md.

Interpretation

The ladder made the egoist converge in 0 of 30 cells where amplification alone did not (cliff exit: 1).

At alpha 1 and outside option 0.6, cliff exit left a policy distance of 0.19; the full ladder leaves 0.50.

Mean rung Shapley on policy distance (positive = closes the gap relative to the cliff): check-in -0.006, renegotiate -0.016, withdraw 0.002.

Withdrawal alone does the work: with only that rung, D is 0.02 at outside option 0.6 and 0.00 at 0.8, with the pair kept. The egoist feels withdrawal because it takes away the amplified shared weeks it values.

Renegotiation undoes it: a demand that applies only on that rung is met by the egoist's next common-ground week, the partner calms down, and the egoist returns to its own passion. Check-in carries no information here (the actor already sees the partner's estimate) and only adds slack.

The withdraw-only finding came out of the rung decomposition, not a headline prediction, and its robustness to noise and blindness was not tested.
